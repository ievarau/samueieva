Our Love Story

Our Story

✿

From our first walk along the Akerselva river to high-mountain hikes in Jotunheimen, our love has always been rooted in adventure, laughter, and a shared appreciation for the quiet moments in nature.

When thinking of our wedding, we wanted to capture two parts of our lives: the grand history of Oslo, where we met and built our home, and the cozy, rustic cabin warmth ("hygge") of a typical Norwegian hytte, where we have spent our happiest weekends by the fireplace.

We are so incredibly excited to exchange our vows at the majestic Oslo Rådhuset and then escape to the woods for a cozy, candle-lit dinner with the people we love most. Thank you for being a part of our journey!
