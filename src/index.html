How the Day (and Week!) Will Unfold

The Plan

✿

We'd love for you to know exactly what to expect, so here's the plan in a nutshell.

On Wednesday, June 9, please meet us at the Rådhuset by 2:30 PM. The ceremony begins at 3:00 PM, followed by a brief photo shoot with family and friends. A taxi will then take everyone to Tryvannstua for the dinner celebration, departing at 4:30 PM.

And the celebration doesn't stop there! We'd love to invite all of our guests to join us afterwards in a rented hytta on the hills near Hamar, from Thursday to Sunday. On Saturday, we'll all head together to the Hamar Medieval Festival. Come for the wedding day, the hytta, or the whole week — whatever works best for you!

Schedule of Events

✿
Wednesday, June 9 — The Wedding Day
3:00 PM (15:00)

Marriage Ceremony at Oslo Rådhuset

Our official marriage ceremony in the majestic main hall of Oslo City Hall, surrounded by historic murals.

5:30 PM (17:30)

Dinner Reception at Tryvannstua

An organized taxi will take guests from Rådhuset to the Tryvannstua forest lodge in Nordmarka for a warm welcome, dinner, speeches, and an evening bonfire under the stars.

Thursday, June 10 — Travel to Hamar
After lunch (14:00)

Drive to Rented Hytte in Hamar

We take cars together after lunch and head up to the rented hytte at Fjellvegen 751, Hamar, Innlandet 2324, settle in, and enjoy a warm evening together.

Friday, June 11 — Relax at the Hytta
All Day

Organized Hiking & Relaxing

A relaxed day at the hytte — an organized hike for those who fancy it, or simply relaxing, chatting, and enjoying the surroundings.

Saturday, June 12 — Medieval Festival
Full Day

Hamar Medieval Festival

Spending the entire day experiencing the famous Hamar Middelalderfestival at Domkirkeodden — featuring historic markets, music, food, and knight tournaments.

Sunday, June 13 — Farewell & Departure
Morning / Afternoon

Return to Oslo & Airport Transfers

Heading back to Oslo or taking transfers to Oslo Airport (OSL Gardermoen) for guests catching Sunday flights home.
